Laptop Battery Finder

Type a laptop model, a brand, a battery code, or a charger part number. Type soldered ram, upgradable ram, wwan or a GPU name to filter by hardware. Results update as you type.

Part numbers come from manufacturer manuals and parts listings. A medium confidence means a parts shop or iFixit listed the code. Check the label inside the laptop before you order.

Data: data/batteries.csv. Press / to jump to the search box.
